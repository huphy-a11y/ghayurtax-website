The dates

Standard CRA and Ontario deadlines.

If a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, the CRA treats it as on time if received the next business day. Corporate deadlines that depend on your year-end are on the Deadline tools page. Last reviewed September 2026.

Employees, families, self-employed, investors.

WhenDeadlineWho it applies toNextAdd
Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec — 15thPersonal tax instalment due
Instalments are required if your net tax owing is more than $3,000 this year and was in either of the two previous years. The CRA sends instalment reminders in February and August.		Self-employed, landlords, investors — anyone who owes more than $3,000 at filingDec 15, 2026Google Calendar
December 31Last day for this year's donations, FHSA and RESP contributions
Charitable donations, FHSA contributions (no 60-day extension) and RESP contributions for this year's grant must be made by December 31. TFSA contributions can be made any time there is room.		EveryoneDec 31, 2026Google Calendar
February last dayT4, T4A and T5 slips arrive
Employers, payers and banks must issue slips by the last day of February. If yours haven't arrived by the first week of March, ask for them.		EveryoneFeb 28, 2027Google Calendar
March 1RRSP contribution deadline for last year's return
Contributions made in the first 60 days of the year can be deducted on the previous year's return (February 29 in a leap year). FHSA contributions do not get the 60-day rule — they must be made by December 31.		RRSP contributorsMar 1, 2027Google Calendar
April 30T1 personal return and balance due
File your T1 and pay any balance. Self-employed people and their spouses have until June 15 to file, but interest on a balance starts May 1.		Everyone (self-employed: payment due today, return by June 15)Apr 30, 2027Google Calendar
June 15T1 filing deadline — self-employed and their spouses
Last day to file without a late-filing penalty. Any balance was due April 30.		Self-employed and spousesJun 15, 2027Google Calendar

Corporations & business Subscribe to this calendar

Corporations, partnerships, trusts, employers.

WhenDeadlineWho it applies toNextAdd
February last dayT4, T4A and T5 slips and summaries due to the CRA
File the T4 and T4A summaries and give employees their slips. T5s for dividends paid during the year are due the same day.		Employers; corporations that paid dividendsFeb 28, 2027Google Calendar
February last dayCorporate tax balance due — December 31 year-ends
Two months after year-end. Canadian-controlled private corporations claiming the small business deduction that meet the income tests get three months (March 31). Other year-ends: use the Deadline tools page on ghayurtax.com.		Corporations with a December 31 year-endFeb 28, 2027Google Calendar
March 15Ontario Employer Health Tax annual return
Due March 15 for the previous calendar year. Employers with Ontario payroll under the exemption usually do not have to file — check with us if unsure.		Employers who file EHTMar 15, 2027Google Calendar
March 31T3 trust returns and T5013 partnership returns
T3 returns and slips are due 90 days after the trust's year-end (March 31 for December year-ends; March 30 in a leap year). T5013 partnership returns are due March 31 for most partnerships.		Trusts with a December year-end; most partnershipsMar 31, 2027Google Calendar
June 30T2 corporate return and Ontario annual return — December 31 year-ends
Six months after year-end for the T2 and for the Ontario annual return filed through the Ontario Business Registry. The tax itself was due in February or March. Other year-ends: use the Deadline tools page on ghayurtax.com.		Corporations with a December 31 year-endJun 30, 2027Google Calendar

Payroll & HST/GST Subscribe to this calendar

Employers and HST registrants.

WhenDeadlineWho it applies toNextAdd
15th of every monthPayroll source deductions due (regular remitters)
CPP, EI and income tax withheld in the previous month. Quarterly remitters pay on April 15, July 15, October 15 and January 15. Accelerated remitters have earlier dates.		Employers remitting monthlyOct 15, 2026Google Calendar
Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct — last dayHST/GST return and payment — quarterly filers
One month after the end of each calendar quarter. Annual filers who owed more than $3,000 last year pay quarterly instalments on the same dates.		Quarterly filers on calendar quarters; annual filers' instalmentsOct 31, 2026Google Calendar
Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct — last dayWSIB premiums and reporting — quarterly reporters
Report insurable earnings and pay premiums by the end of the month after each quarter.		Ontario employers reporting to WSIB quarterlyOct 31, 2026Google Calendar
March 31HST/GST annual return and payment — corporations with a December 31 year-end
Three months after the fiscal year-end for annual filers. Other year-ends: use the Deadline tools page on ghayurtax.com.		Corporations filing HST annuallyMar 31, 2027Google Calendar
April 30HST/GST payment due — self-employed annual filers
The payment is due April 30 even though the return is not due until June 15.		Sole proprietors filing HST annually with a December 31 year-endApr 30, 2027Google Calendar
June 15HST/GST annual return — self-employed annual filers
The return is due June 15; the payment was due April 30.		Sole proprietors filing HST annually with a December 31 year-endJun 15, 2027Google Calendar

Childcare funding (CWELCC) Subscribe to this calendar

Licensed childcare centres — Peel dates; Halton and Toronto differ.

WhenDeadlineWho it applies toNextAdd
September 30Third-quarter spending check against your allocation
Last realistic point to change staffing, professional learning and eligible purchases before year-end.		All CWELCC centres (recommended)Sep 30, 2026Google Calendar
December 31Last day this year's eligible costs count
Unspent allocation is recovered at reconciliation. Professional learning budgets and planned eligible purchases must be spent and documented by today.		All CWELCC centresDec 31, 2026Google Calendar
January 30Planning time and professional learning notices to staff (Peel)
Tell staff in writing how planning time (four paid hours a month) and professional learning (up to $500 per full-time equivalent) will work this year. Proof goes to the Region by February 13. Halton and Toronto set their own dates.		CWELCC centres in PeelJan 30, 2027Google Calendar
February 13Proof of planning time and professional learning to Peel Region
Submit the staff communication and plan to the Region.		CWELCC centres in PeelFeb 13, 2027Google Calendar
February last dayT4s reconciled to GOF, WEG and WCF enhancements
Wage enhancements must be shown separately on pay stubs and agree with the funding reports and the T4s filed today.		All CWELCC centresFeb 28, 2027Google Calendar
April 30Audited financial statements and FAIR due to Peel Region
Toronto: audited statements within four months of year-end, plus the Standardized Financial Reports on the City's schedule. Halton: check the Region's guideline.		CWELCC centres in PeelApr 30, 2027Google Calendar
June 30Half-year spending check against your allocation
Compare actual eligible spending and base-fee revenue to the allocation while there is time to adjust. Unspent allocation and ineligible costs are recovered at reconciliation.		All CWELCC centres (recommended)Jun 30, 2027Google Calendar
