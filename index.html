How it works

Three steps, no hand-offs.

  1. Step 1

    Call or email

    A short conversation about the business or the household, what's due, and what you want to change. You'll get a straight answer on fit and fee.

  2. Step 2

    Upload securely

    We send an Intuit Link invitation. You upload slips, statements and receipts from a phone or computer — no email attachments, no paper unless you prefer it.

Common questions

Before you call.

Do you handle both the corporation and the owner's personal return?

Yes. For incorporated professionals and business owners we usually prepare the T2 and the family's T1s together, so salary, dividends and personal credits are planned as one picture.

Can you deal with the CRA on my behalf?

Yes. Once you authorize us as your representative, we can respond to CRA notices, reviews and requests for information for you.

How do I send my documents safely?

Through Intuit Link, our secure client portal. Existing clients sign in and upload from a phone or computer; new clients ask us for an invitation by phone or email and we send one the same day. Please don't email your SIN, banking details or ID.

Do I need to come into the office?

No. Most of our work is done remotely, and we work with clients across Canada. If you'd rather meet in person, call or email to arrange a time at the Mississauga office.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer — the details are on the Pay an invoice page. Put your name or business name and the invoice number in the message so we can match it.
