CWELCC guide · 2026 guidelines

CWELCC deadlines for Peel, Halton and Toronto centres.

Each Region sets its own CWELCC dates, and missing one can hold up funding or trigger a recovery. These are the dates a licensed centre needs on the calendar, with the payroll and tax deadlines that fall in the same weeks.

A calendar that works

January: set the spending plan against the new allocation; confirm wage floors and enhancement stacking; send staff notices.

February to April: close the prior year, prepare the reconciliation and the FAIR or SFRs, and get the file to the auditor early. Auditors are busiest in March.

May to September: quarterly check of spending against allocation and base-fee revenue against expected.

October to December: use the remaining eligible budget, including professional learning, and model next year's staffing costs.

Quick answers

What operators ask about deadlines.

What happens if the audited statements are late?

The Region can hold payments or start a recovery until the package is in. Ask for an extension in writing before the deadline, not after.

Do Peel, Halton and Toronto use the same forms?

No. Peel uses the FAIR, Toronto uses Standardized Financial Reports, and Halton has its own package. The underlying numbers are the same, which is why site-level books matter more than the form.

How many closure days can a CWELCC centre have?

Since January 1, 2026, a maximum of 20 planned paid closure days a year, no more than two weeks in a row or four weeks in a year. Tell the Region in writing before increasing your closure days, because the change can affect funding.

Can we add these dates to our calendar?

Yes. The key dates page has a calendar you subscribe to once, including the childcare funding dates.
