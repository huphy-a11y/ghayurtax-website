CWELCC guide · 2026 guidelines

2026 wage floors, WEG and workforce compensation, explained.

In 2026 the CWELCC wage floor is $25.86 an hour for RECE program staff and $26.86 for RECE supervisors. Workforce funding is reconciled separately from program costs, and it is recovered if it was not paid to staff the way the guideline requires.

Why the order matters

Each layer is calculated on the result of the one before it. Applying them out of order overstates what the Region owes, and it shows up at reconciliation as a recovery.

The steps on this page follow section 5.3 of Peel Region's 2026 centre-based guideline. Other Regions follow the same provincial order.

The order of operations

Six layers, applied in sequence.

  1. Start with the employer-paid wage

    The base wage the employer paid as of December 31, 2025.

  2. Add GOF wage enhancements

    The general operating funding hourly enhancements carried from 2024.

  3. Add the Wage Enhancement Grant

    Up to $2 an hour, to a ceiling of $33.81.

  4. RECEs: add the workforce compensation increase

    Up to $4 an hour, if the total is still below $28 for program staff or $31 for supervisors.

  5. RECEs: apply the wage floor

    $25.86 for program staff or $26.86 for supervisors.

Payroll rules that get tested

What the Region checks on your pay stubs.

  • Every cheque. Enhancements are paid each pay period, not saved for a year-end lump sum.
  • Shown separately. Pay stubs show the enhancement lines separately from base wages.
  • Eligible staff only. Floors apply to RECE program staff and supervisors; WEG has its own eligibility list. Track non-RECE and ineligible hours separately.
  • Benefits follow wages. Mandatory employer costs on the enhancement (CPP, EI, vacation pay, EHT, WSIB) are part of the claim; keep the calculation.
  • Tell staff in writing. Peel asks for staff notices by January 30 and proof by February 13.

At year-end

The T4s, the payroll register and the workforce lines in the Region's return have to agree. If an employee left mid-year, changed roles or registered with the College of ECEs part-way through, document the date, because eligibility changes on that date.

Quick answers

What operators ask about wages.

What is the CWELCC wage floor in 2026?

$25.86 an hour for RECE program staff and $26.86 an hour for RECE supervisors.

Does the wage floor apply to non-RECE staff?

No. The floors are for RECE program staff and supervisors. Non-RECE staff may still qualify for the Wage Enhancement Grant.

Can we pay above the ceiling?

Yes, from the centre's own budget. Funding only covers up to the ceiling.

What if we underpaid during the year?

Correct it through payroll before December 31 where possible, with retroactive pay shown on the stub. Unpaid workforce funding is recovered, not carried forward.

Source: Peel Region 2026 CWELCC centre-based guideline, sections 5.3 to 5.7 and 6. See also the reconciliation, step by step.
