Payments

Pay an invoice by Interac e-Transfer.

The simplest way to pay, from any Canadian bank. Two details in the message and we match it the same day.

  1. Open Interac e-Transfer in your online banking

    Add a new recipient: Ghayur Accounts and Tax Services.

  2. Send to office@ghayurtax.com

    Use this address exactly. It is the only e-Transfer address we use.

  3. Put two things in the message

    Your name or business name, and the invoice number. That's what lets us match the payment to your file.

  4. We confirm by email

    You'll get a note from office@ghayurtax.com once it's received and applied.

Good to know

Corporate invoices: send from the business account so the payment matches the corporation's invoice.

Over your bank's daily limit? Split it across two days, or call or text and we'll arrange another way.

Security question: if your bank requires one, text the answer to 647-870-8498 rather than emailing it.

We will never change this address by email. If any email or message asks you to send a payment somewhere else, call 647-870-8498 before paying.

A question about an invoice?

Email office@ghayurtax.com with the invoice number, or call or text 647-870-8498.
