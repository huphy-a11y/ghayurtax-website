Why an accounting firm

Because the numbers tell us where the hours go.

Most AI projects fail because they start with the tool. We start with your books: where staff time is spent, which tasks repeat every month, and what an hour saved is worth. That is how we decide what is worth automating.

  • Daycares: enrolment and fee tracking, funding reports drafted from the books, staff-hour summaries for the wage rules.
  • Medical practices: billing reconciliations, expense capture, and month-end reports for the professional corporation.
  • Small businesses: invoice and receipt intake, cash-flow forecasts, client onboarding, internal training.

How we handle your data

Common questions

What owners ask about AI.

Is AI bookkeeping accurate enough to rely on?

With review, yes. AI is very good at reading statements and sorting transactions, and it makes different mistakes than people do. We use it for the first pass and a person checks every reconciliation, so you get the speed without giving up the judgment.

Is my financial data safe with AI tools?

We use business-grade tools that do not train on client data, keep files in our secure portal, and keep SINs, banking details and ID out of AI systems entirely.

What does an AI project cost?

A fixed fee agreed before we start, based on the work. Many first projects are small: a strategy session, or one process automated. Call 647-870-8498 for a quote.

Do I need to be an accounting client to use your AI services?

No. Some clients come for an AI project only. Most stay for the books, because clean numbers are what make the automation pay.
